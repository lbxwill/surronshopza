Baja Light Installation Tutorial

Safety first: switch the bike off, remove the key, and disconnect the battery before working on the wiring. Brackets and wire colours can differ between Sur-Ron models and light kits. Follow the instructions supplied with your kit and ask a qualified adult or bike technician for help if you are unsure.

Tools you may need

Allen keys, small spanners or sockets, cable ties, electrical tape or heat-shrink, and the bracket and wiring harness supplied with the light.

Installation steps

  1. Prepare the bike. Park it securely, switch it off, remove the key, and disconnect the battery.
  2. Remove the original light or plate. Support it while loosening the bolts. Do not pull on the wires.
  3. Fit the Baja-light bracket. Align it with the front mounting points and start every bolt by hand.
  4. Mount the light. Attach the light to the bracket, but leave it slightly loose for aiming.
  5. Connect the harness. Use the supplied plug and never force a connector.
  6. Secure the wiring. Keep it away from the tyre, brake hose, steering movement, sharp edges, and hot parts.
  7. Reconnect and test. Reconnect the battery, switch the bike on, and test the light.
  8. Check steering movement. Turn the handlebars fully left and right to make sure the wire does not stretch.
  9. Aim the beam. Point it slightly downward so it lights the road without dazzling other people.
  10. Final check. Tighten the bolts and check everything again after the first short ride.

Do not connect bare wires straight to the battery unless the kit specifically supports it and includes the correct fuse, switch, voltage rating, and wiring instructions.
