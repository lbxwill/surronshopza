About the website

Sur-Ron Shop is an online parts store focused on simple upgrades that make your bike look better and work better. The website currently sells a Baja light, an underglow kit and a vented plate.

🛒 What we sell

Baja Light — R500
Underglow Kit — R350
Vented Plate — R700
Duck with Spinning Hat — R200 preorder

📦 How ordering works

Add products to your cart, enter your shipping details and submit the order. You will then be contacted to confirm stock, delivery and payment.

🔒 Payments

The website does not collect card numbers, CVV codes, PINs or banking passwords. Payment must be arranged through a trusted and secure method.

📱 Contact and social

🛠️ Installation safety

Switch the bike off and disconnect the battery before electrical work. Ask a qualified adult or bike technician for help if you are unsure.

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. Orders are sent through Formspree to the email connected to the form.
